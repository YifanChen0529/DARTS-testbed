Empirical CDFs of per-release response time divided by relative deadline for B3 (left panel) and B4 (right panel). Solid curves are the admitted configurations; density increases left to right with darker shade (achieved factored bottleneck densities 0.2997, 0.4990, 0.6967, 0.8448 on B3 and 0.2998, 0.4989, 0.6986, 0.8462 on B4). The dashed curve is the configuration outside the certified region (density 1.0730 on B3, 1.0726 on B4). The dotted curve is the densest admitted configuration at a second evaluation seed. Each curve pools 120 job releases (4 classes times 30 releases). The vertical line marks the relative deadline (ratio 1). The horizontal axis is compressed between 0.72 and 0.96; no plotted job release falls in the omitted interval.
